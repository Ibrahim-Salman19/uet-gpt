# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students, staff, and faculty of the University of Engineering and Technology (UET) Taxila using UETGPT to query campus info, academic syllabi, official documents, directories, and schedules. They expect fast, accurate, and professional academic answers.

## Product Purpose
To serve as an authoritative, high-fidelity AI-powered companion for UET Taxila, providing RAG-enhanced campus and academic guidance with absolute clarity and professional visual polish.

## Brand Personality
Neo Kinpaku: expert, decisive, refined. Dark urushi lacquer backdrops with precise kinpaku gold hairlines and verdigris patina accent cues. Restrained, technical, and premium.

## Anti-references
- Generic ChatGPT wrappers with standard Tailwind gray/slate backgrounds.
- High-saturation gradients or purple/cyan cyber-glows.
- Over-rounded card borders (radii ≥ 24px).
- Saturated beige/cream backgrounds.
- Decorative motion that doesn't convey state.

## Design Principles
1. **Urushi Dark Lacquer Surfaces**: Utilize dark warm-black mineral backdrops to ground the interface and reduce cognitive fatigue.
2. **Kinpaku Gold Hairline Structure**: Use thin, high-contrast gold rules to define layouts and boundaries instead of cards or heavy shadows.
3. **Verdigris Patina State Accents**: Reserve green verdigris only for success, connection status, and active system signals.
4. **Typographic Restraint**: Rely on clean sans-serif and mono faces at fixed scales to prioritize dense, readable academic text.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratios verified at ≥ 4.5:1.
- Clear, readable sans-serif text.
- Full keyboard navigability with visible focus indicators.
- Animation-free alternatives for prefers-reduced-motion.
