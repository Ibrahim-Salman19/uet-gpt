# Marketing Ideas — UET GPT

_Date: 2026-07-16 · Skill: marketing-ideas_

## Highest-leverage, low-cost ideas (free open-source tool)
1. **Admissions-season timing** — launch hard in May-Aug when "UET Taxila admissions" volume peaks.
2. **WhatsApp/PDF export** — let students export answers as PDF (shareable in groups) → viral loop.
3. **"Ask about my merit" calculator** — ECAT/HSSC/SSC merit estimator (interactive) → link bait + citations.
4. **GitHub star campaign** — "If UET GPT helped your admission, star the repo."
5. **Collaborate with UET societies** — IEEE/CS societies share to members.
6. **YouTube explainer** — "How to get into UET Taxila 2025" (cited by Google AI Overviews).
7. **Quora answers** — answer "How is UET Taxila?" with a link (Quora cited by AI).
8. **Local SEO** — Google Business Profile for UET Taxila entity (helps Knowledge Graph).

## Ideas to deprioritize (don't fit free tool)
- Paid ads, cold email, sales outreach, referral incentives (no budget / no sales motion).

## Test one at a time
Pick the merit calculator + admissions-season push first; measure referral traffic.
