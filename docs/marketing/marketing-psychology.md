# Marketing Psychology — UET GPT

_Date: 2026-07-16 · Skill: marketing-psychology_

## Principles that apply to a student-focused free tool
1. **Reduction of uncertainty (relief):** Admissions is anxiety-driven. Lead with "Get a clear answer in seconds" — speaks to the core emotional job.
2. **Authority via source:** "Answers from official UET Taxila documents" leverages trust in the institution (vs. gossip groups).
3. **Free = removal of friction:** Emphasize $0 and open source (no signup barrier to trust).
4. **Social proof (when available):** "Used by UET Taxila students" — powerful for a campus audience (bandwagon).
5. **Reciprocity:** Free tool + open source → users more likely to star/contribute/share.
6. **Loss aversion:** "Don't miss your merit-list deadline" — timely, admissions-season messaging.

## Applied to copy
- Hero: relief + specificity ("answers from official data, not guesses").
- CTAs: low-commitment ("Ask now", not "Sign up").
- Timing: urgency during ECAT/merit-list windows.

## Anti-patterns
- Fear-mongering ("you'll fail without us") — damages trust, off-brand.
- Overclaiming — academic-integrity sensitive audience.
