# Community Marketing — UET GPT

_Date: 2026-07-16 · Skill: community-marketing_

## Goal
Turn UET Taxila students into advocates who share UET GPT and contribute data/corrections — building both authority and a feedback loop.

## Community Surfaces
- **Reddit:** r/UET (official-style presence), r/Islamabad
- **GitHub:** issues + discussions for corrections/feature requests (open source lever)
- **WhatsApp/Facebook groups:** existing unofficial UET groups (partner, don't spam)
- **Discord/Slack:** optional future student community

## Tactics
1. **Open a GitHub Discussion** "Help us improve UET GPT answers" — crowdsource document corrections.
2. **Contributor invite:** "Add a document / fix an answer" — leverages open-source license.
3. **Reddit AMA-style thread** during admissions season (May-Aug).
4. **Student ambassador:** recruit 1-2 UET students to validate answers pre-launch.
5. **Feedback widget** on the site (low-effort, builds trust + data).

## Why this helps rankings
- Community discussions on Reddit are cited by ChatGPT (~1.8% of citations) and Perplexity.
- GitHub activity + stars = authority signal for AI systems.
- Real student testimonials = E-E-A-T (experience) for traditional SEO.

## Caution
Authentic participation only. No astroturfing, no buying reviews. Disclose open source.
