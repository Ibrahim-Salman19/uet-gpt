# Content Strategy — UET GPT

_Date: 2026-07-16 · Skill: content-strategy_

## Goal
Own the "UET" / "UET Taxila" information space in both traditional search and AI answers, targeting prospective + current UET Taxila students and their parents.

## Audience & Intent
| Segment | Top queries | Intent |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Prospective students | "UET Taxila admissions", "UET Taxila fee structure", "UET Taxila merit" | Research / decide |
| Parents | "UET Taxila ranking", "UET Taxila vs NUST fees" | Evaluate |
| Current students | "UET Taxila date sheet", "UET Taxila transport" | Navigational / info |
| General public | "what is UET Taxila", "UET full form" | Informational |

## Content Pillars (topical clusters)
1. **Admissions & Entry** — ECAT, eligibility, merit formula, documents, merit lists.
2. **Academics** — 14 departments, 6 faculties, programs (UG/PG/PhD), scope.
3. **Cost & Aid** — fee structure, hostel charges, scholarships, refund policy.
4. **Campus Life** — transport, hostels, societies, location.
5. **Comparisons** — UET Taxila vs peer universities.
6. **Glossary** — ECAT, HEC, Washington Accord, merit, etc.

## Existing Assets (leverage)
- 6 live pages (home, uet-taxila hub, admissions, programs, fee-structure, uet-gpt).
- RAG corpus from official docs = proprietary source for all content.
- `llms.txt`, `pricing.md`, JSON-LD already live.

- AI-native: ensure llms.txt + schema feed ChatGPT/Perplexity/Claude.

## KPIs
- Indexed pages (target: 6 → 30+).
- `site:uet-gpt.vercel.app` results count.
- Branded query rankings ("UET GPT", "UET Taxila AI").
- Citations in Perplexity/ChatGPT for "UET Taxila" queries (monthly DIY check).

## Governance
- All factual claims sourced from official UET Taxila material (prospectus/website).
- Refresh admissions/fee content each admissions cycle (annual).
- "Last updated" date on every public page.
